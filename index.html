What is the FNFTA?

The First Nations Financial Transparency Act (2013) requires bands under the Indian Act to publicly post audited financial statements and a Schedule of Remuneration and Expenses for all Chief and Council members each year.

🔎

What does OpenBand show?

We index the ISC public registry so you can search any First Nation by name, see all available fiscal years, and access direct links to the official PDF filings — remuneration schedules and audited financial statements.

🤝

Who built this?

OpenBand is a community transparency project. All data comes directly from Indigenous Services Canada's public FNFTA registry. No data is modified or interpreted — we just make it easier to find.
