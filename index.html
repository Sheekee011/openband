What is the FNFTA?

The First Nations Financial Transparency Act requires First Nations to publish audited financial statements and Chief & Council remuneration schedules on ISC's website.

🔎

What does OpenBand show?

Real remuneration and expense figures parsed directly from FNFTA filings. If a filing hasn't been parsed yet, we link directly to the ISC PDF so you can view it yourself.

⚠️

Data availability

Not every band has filed for every year. Some PDFs may be unavailable or not yet processed by the nightly scraper. Always verify against the original ISC filing.
