What is the FNFTA?

The First Nations Financial Transparency Act requires public audited remuneration schedules for Chief and Council compensation.

🔎

What does OpenBand show?

Salary, remuneration, and expense figures for each Chief and Councillor. Estimated figures are clearly labelled.

🤝

Who built this?

OpenBand is a community transparency prototype using public Indigenous Services Canada filing links.
